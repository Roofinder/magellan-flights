# Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) — Magellan Flights

SEO brings people to the page; CRO turns them into money. For a plumber, a
"conversion" is a lead form or phone call. **For us, a conversion is a click on an
Aviasales booking link** (marker 741311) — that's the only way the site earns. So
CRO here = maximizing qualified booking-link clicks without ever faking a deal.

## The conversion goal
Get the visitor from "is this real?" to "I'm clicking Book" as fast as possible,
on every page — blog posts, money pages, and deal cards.

## Principles we apply (battle-tested, not the video's paywalled blueprint)
1. **Lead with value, fast.** A real number or a live deal above the fold. No
   throat-clearing (ties to the writing-style guide).
2. **One obvious primary action per screen.** A high-contrast gold "Book / See live
   price" button. Don't bury it; repeat it after long content (we already double the
   CTA in articles).
3. **Trust, because it's their money.** Say it plainly: "real, re-checked fares,"
   "tracked daily," "click goes to a secure booking page." Verification badges where
   we have them. People click when they believe the price.
4. **Urgency that's honest.** "Fares change fast — book soon if you love it." True
   for airfare, so it converts without lying.
5. **Scannable.** Short paragraphs, clear headers, tables/lists. A wall of text
   doesn't convert.
6. **Remove friction.** Booking opens in a new tab (rel=noopener), keeps our page
   alive, no surprise interstitials.
7. **Match intent.** A "cheap flights from Boston" visitor wants BOS deals first,
   not a generic pitch.

## Where it's applied
- **Blog posts:** answer box up top, two CTAs to the live board, internal links.
- **Money/matrix pages:** the live cheapest fares for that origin ARE the conversion
  surface — price + dates + a Book button per row, sorted cheapest-first.
- **Deal cards / ticker:** clear price, % off, and a Book button.

## Measure later
Once Search Console + any click analytics exist, watch booking-link CTR by page and
A/B the CTA copy/placement. Until then, apply the principles above by default.
