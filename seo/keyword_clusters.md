# Keyword clusters → blog posts

Each cluster = **one blog post** targeting a **primary** keyword + **4 secondary**
keywords (the video's "take 5 shots instead of 1"). Every post links to the money
page in *Internal link* so authority flows to pages that earn.

Ranked by priority (volume × winnability × how well our live data answers it).

---

## 1. booking-day  ⭐ write first
- **Primary:** cheapest day to book flights (12,100 / KD 33)
- Secondary: cheapest day to fly · what day of the week are flights cheapest ·
  best day to buy plane tickets · is tuesday the cheapest day to fly
- **Intent:** Informational · **Title:** "The Cheapest Day to Book Flights (What 1,000s of Real Fares Show)"
- **Why first:** high volume, strong buyer intent, and we can answer it with our
  *own* live price data — something competitors can't fake.
- **Internal link →** market.html
