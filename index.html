WATCH

WAIT

Why “Magellan”?

In 1519, Ferdinand Magellan set sail to prove the world could be crossed — and his expedition became the first to circle the entire globe. It was the moment the planet became reachable. Five centuries on, the world is more connected than ever, yet the cost of seeing it is wrapped in mystery and made to feel out of reach. Magellan Flights exists to pull back that curtain: to put the real numbers in your hands, show you that a week abroad can cost less than a weekend at home, and give the freedom of travel back to ordinary people.
